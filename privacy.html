Privacy Policy

What we collect, and how we use it

Information We Collect

  • Name, phone number, and delivery address — collected at checkout to fulfill your order.
  • Order history and items purchased.
  • Products you save to your wishlist, stored locally on your device.

How We Use It

  • To process, confirm, and deliver your orders via our courier partners.
  • To contact you about your order through WhatsApp or phone.
  • To improve the products and categories we stock.

What We Don't Do

We don't sell or rent your personal information to third parties. Your details are used only to fulfil and support your own orders.

Third Parties

Order and delivery details are shared with our courier partner solely to complete delivery. Order confirmation is sent through WhatsApp, which is subject to WhatsApp's own privacy terms.

Data Storage

Order records are kept to handle returns, exchanges, and customer support. Wishlist ("Saved") items are stored in your browser's local storage and are not sent to us unless you place an order.

Your Choices

You can ask us to delete your order history or personal details by contacting us directly. Clearing your browser data will remove any locally saved wishlist items.

Questions?

Reach out on WhatsApp or email fashionistabd.ceo@gmail.com.
