Return & Exchange Policy

Applies to all orders placed on fashion1sta.com

7-Day Return Window

You can request a return or exchange within 7 days of receiving your order. Requests made after this window can't be accepted.

Eligibility

  • The item must be unused, unworn, and in its original condition with tags attached.
  • The item must be in its original packaging.
  • You'll need your order number or the phone number used to place the order.

Non-Returnable Items

  • Items marked as final sale.
  • Items damaged due to misuse after delivery.
  • Fake piercings, earrings, and grillz — for hygiene reasons — unless received defective or incorrect.

How to Request a Return or Exchange

  • Message us on WhatsApp with your order number and reason for return.
  • We'll confirm pickup or drop-off details within 24 hours.
  • Once we receive and inspect the item, we'll process your exchange or refund.

Refunds

Approved refunds are issued as store credit or via the original payment method (cash on delivery orders are refunded via mobile banking). Refunds are processed within 3–5 business days after we receive the returned item.

Exchanges

Want a different size or color instead? Let us know when you reach out — we'll arrange the swap once the original item is received back in acceptable condition, subject to availability.

Damaged or Wrong Item Received

If your order arrives damaged or you received the wrong item, contact us within 7 days with photos — we'll arrange a free replacement or full refund, no questions asked.

Questions?

Reach out on WhatsApp or email fashionistabd.ceo@gmail.com.
