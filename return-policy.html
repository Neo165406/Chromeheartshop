Return & Exchange Policy

Please inspect your parcel in the presence of the delivery person before accepting delivery.

Eligible issues

Contact us if your order arrives damaged or defective, contains the wrong product, or has an incorrect size.

How to report a problem

  1. Inspect the parcel and product as soon as it arrives.
  2. Take clear photos of the product and parcel showing the issue.
  3. If possible, report the issue to the delivery person and return the affected item immediately.
  4. Send the photos and order details to our contact team. We will review the claim and, if eligible, arrange an exchange.

Reporting window

If you could not identify the issue at delivery, report a damaged or defective product, incorrect product or incorrect size within 7 days of receiving the parcel. Claims reported after 7 days from delivery are not eligible under this policy.

Return shipping fees

For a confirmed defective, damaged or incorrect item, FASHIONISTA covers the return shipping cost. For other returns, the customer is responsible for return shipping fees. Eligibility is reviewed against the issue reported and the product condition.

Refunds and exchanges

For eligible problems covered by this policy, FASHIONISTA will arrange an exchange. Contact us before sending an item back so our team can explain the next steps. This policy does not promise a cash refund where an exchange is the stated resolution.
