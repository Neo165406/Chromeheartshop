Return & Exchange Policy

Important: Please inspect your parcel carefully in the presence of the delivery person before accepting the delivery.

If you notice any of the following issues at the time of delivery:

  • Damaged or defective product
  • Incorrect product
  • Incorrect size

Please follow these steps:

  1. Take clear photos of the issue immediately while the delivery person is present.
  2. Return the affected product immediately to the delivery person.
  3. Send us the photos as proof of the issue.
  4. If eligible, we will arrange an exchange parcel for you.

Important Notice

Once the parcel has been accepted and the delivery person has left, we will not be able to accept any claims regarding product damage, incorrect product, or incorrect size.

Thank you for your cooperation and understanding.
